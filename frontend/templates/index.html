THE SCIENCE OF SPEED

How TypeBlaze scores you

Gross WPM

Characters typed ÷ 5 ÷ elapsed minutes. A standard word is five characters.

Net WPM

Gross WPM minus incorrect characters ÷ 5. The result cannot fall below zero.

Accuracy

Correct character positions ÷ all submitted characters × 100, including extra characters.
